# Sign in

**Previous:** none. **Next:** [Create a school](create-a-school.md) (master tenant) or [Add a campus](add-a-campus.md) (school administrator).

## What this does

Signs you in to the portal on your school's own web address (its domain). After the first sign-in you may be asked to choose your own password.

## Who can do it

Anyone who has an account. Accounts are created by staff; there is no public sign-up. A school can also be registered from the platform's own address, see [Create a school](create-a-school.md).

## Before you start

- You need the **email** and **password** your school gave you.
- Open the **school's domain**, for example `school1.com`. A school's login page is shown in the school's own colours and logo.
- If your school has no domain yet, it signs in on the platform's domain with its email and password and acts as its own school there.

## Steps

1. Open the school's domain in a browser.
2. On the **Sign in** page, type your **Email** in the *Email* field.
3. Type your **Password** in the *Password* field.
4. Select **Sign in**.
5. You see the home page with the text *You are signed in as* followed by your email.

If you were given a one-time password, you are sent to **Choose your password** (the page title). Type your current password, then your new one, then repeat the new one, and select **Save password**. You see the home page.

To change the language, use the language dropdown on the sign-in page. The page then reloads in the chosen language.

## What each right allows

Signing in gives no rights by itself. Your role and your campuses decide what you may do after you sign in (see [Create a role](create-a-role.md) and [Create a user](create-a-user.md)).

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | Cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| *Invalid email or password.* | The email or password is wrong. The message is the same whether or not the account exists. | Check the spelling. Ask your school administrator to reset your password if you are sure it is right. |
| *Too many sign-in attempts. Wait a minute and try again.* | Too many attempts in a short time. | Wait a minute, then try again. |
| You are sent to **Choose your password** | You signed in with a one-time password. | Choose your own password before anything else. |
| The page does not load, or the school's logo is missing | The address is not a school's domain, or the school is inactive. | Check the address with your school. An unknown address is refused. |
| *Sign-in failed. Reload the page and try again.* | A temporary error. | Reload the page and try again. |

## Worked example

Ada Example works at `Example School`, whose domain is `school1.example.test` (an example address). She opens the address, types `ada.example@example.test` and her password, and selects **Sign in**. Her administrator gave her a one-time password, so she is sent to **Choose your password**, chooses a new one of at least 15 characters, and selects **Save password**.

## Flow recap

*Your school's domain, then your email and password, then your own password if you were given a one-time one.*
